Maybe I should have titled this "Don't Make Slop."

Nobody wants to read a bunch of AI content. And if they did, they could just prompt it themselves.

Instead you want to leverage AI where it shines, but slip into manual mode where a human touch is needed.

## Unreasonable Things

The power of AI is that it is capable of doing unreasonable things.

I like to explain this using a Penn and Teller quote about magic: "Sometimes magic is just someone spending more time on something than anyone else might reasonably expect."

The same holds true for doing powerful things with AI: you want to do things that seem unreasonable and that are only possible because of AI. 

 - use AI to help you plan and scaffold. 
 - use a committee of ai agents from different providers or with different prompts to get different perspectives.
 - when writing an article like this have AI research EVERYTHING point-by-point.
 - build 20 throw away prototypes.

 Find ways to leverage AI than allow you to make something or do something that would not be possible if you had to just do it yourself. 

 ## Don't Be a Passenger

At least for the near term, you will get the best results by finding ways to work with AI that allow you to either "drive" or "navigate". You need to be involved in the planning and execution.

(My big statement on Fable when it came out was that it can drive and navigate for you, it doesn't need a humam to accomplish complicated goals.)

Fable or not, quality of whatever you are producing will suffer if you allow AI to do both the work and the planning without human guidance.

You need to recognize when you're generating poor quality content or writing 50 pages of text when 2 would do.
