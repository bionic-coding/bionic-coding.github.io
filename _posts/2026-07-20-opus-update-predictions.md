Today Opus 4.8 failed multiple times on a task that it normally excels at. The last time this happened we were days away from an Opus update. Barring government intervention, I would expect Opus 5 to ship soon.

## Opus Has a Bad Day

Opus 4.8 shipped May 28, 2026. In the 10 or so days leading up to the release I noticed the then-current version of Opus performing worse.

It's like the model is having a bad day.

My best guess is that when they prep an Opus release an infrastructure change degrades performance for the current version. I've suspected this before with Sonnet as well.

## Timing is Everything

The timing is suspicious. Kimi K3 weights land next week, Qwen 3.8 Max is in the news, and GPT-5.6 Sol launched the week prior. I have to believe that Anthropic is hoping to release model updates to once again cement itself as the leader in AI.

The only caveat is that government intervention could delay the release.

We'll see...
