## Headlines

**1. `Agenticly` is not a word, and it is currently your homepage.**
`garden-improvement-homepage-hero-copy` · The uncommitted edit in `index.md` reads
`hero_title: "Building Agenticly with Durable Intent."` The adverb of *agentic* is
**agentically**. It is one letter short of a word that does exist, which is the kind of typo
a spellcheck skips and a reader does not.

The good news is the timing. It is uncommitted, so it has not deployed. The live site still
carries the old hero. You can fix this before it is ever seen, which is the only reason this
leads tonight over things that are already public.

While you are in there, one editorial note. The thesis now appears twice on the same page in
two shapes: the hero says "Durable Intent", the footer says "Durable intent, disposable
code.", and the browser tab and meta description say the same as the footer. Your own rule
one is *one name per thing*. The lede change itself I would defend — "A practical guide to
building software with AI" is shorter and plainer than what it replaced, and the thesis moved
up into the title rather than being lost. The collision is between the new title and the
four other places the old phrasing already lives.

**2. You now publish three kinds of thing, and only two of them have a contract.**
`garden-idea-field-notes-stream` · Nine posts. Four are "This Week in AI". Five are not, and
two of those five shipped on consecutive days this week. The column has
[[briefs/BRIEF-this-week-in-ai-format]] and a template. Lessons have `order`, `summary`,
`status`, `updated`, and a freshness review. Field notes have nothing.

Three concrete edges, all checkable today:

- `articles.md` describes itself as "Weekly-ish news" and "what's new and why it matters".
  A reader arriving for that finds a Gatekeeper debugging walkthrough second in the list.
- Every post carries `tags:` — `[model-news, weekly]` on the four issues, `[claude-code, zed]`
  and `[local-models, qwen]` on the new ones. Nothing in `_layouts/` or any page reads
  `site.tags`. You are writing a taxonomy and rendering none of it. One post, `my-ai-kit`,
  carries `tags: []`.
- Field notes age differently from both neighbours. Your column brief makes a point that an
  issue is a dated snapshot whose links are *allowed* to rot. Lessons are the inverse and get
  reviewed. A field note is a third case: the finding stays true while the fix path rots fast.
  A vendored adapter path and a Homebrew Node version are exactly the parts that move.

I wrote the options up as [[inbox/gardener-idea-field-notes-stream]] — four of them, cheapest
first, from "decide to do nothing" through "render the tags you already write" to "a `_notes/`
collection with a `verified:` date". It is exploration, not a decision. If you pick anything
past option A it touches
[[adrs/ADR-0005-serve-a-landing-homepage-and-a-five-item-top-nav]] and wants a brief first.

**3. The sharing lesson is good, and it is now one bullet short.**
`garden-research-skill-scan-step` · [[_lessons/sharing-agents-and-skills]] handles the hard
part well. It names prompt injection, least privilege, provenance, verified publishers,
version pinning, and revocation. I went looking for a gap and did not find the one I
expected.

What it lacks is a **scan step**. Its "Installing safely" list is six manual acts — read it,
review the permissions, check provenance. That was the whole toolkit on 2026-07-17 when the
lesson was last reviewed. It is not the whole toolkit now: Anthropic shipped beta skill and
plugin security scanning in early August, and several third-party scanners exist. There is
also a widely repeated research figure — prompt injection found in 36% of scanned agent
skills — that would make a strong opening sentence.

Two honest caveats. Every source for this came from search snippets, not reads, because none
of those domains sit in `sources.md`. And the 36% carries a February date while being cited
in August. Verify both before either reaches a post. Details and the raw snippets are in
[[inbox/gardener-news-agent-skill-scanning]].
